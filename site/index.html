Video in your terminal.
Pixels, not ASCII soup.

framefeed is a terminal video player and a tiny streaming server. It decodes .mp4/.webm/HLS and paints real pixels with Kitty graphics, Sixel or truecolor half-blocks — sized to your actual window, with a quality knob.

Play a bundled clip

$ framefeed serve # bundled clips at /<name>
$ curl -N 'http://127.0.0.1:8080/sintel'
$ curl -N 'http://127.0.0.1:8080/sprite-fright?q=240p&backend=sixel'

Or any source

$ framefeed play ./clip.mp4 --quality 480p --backend auto
$ framefeed play https://host/master.m3u8 --quality 720p --stats
$ curl -N 'http://127.0.0.1:8080/stream?url=./clip.mp4&q=144p'
